# Privacy policy: Tabwise

*Last updated: 7 November 2026*

**Short version: Tabwise never sends anything anywhere. It has no servers and makes no network requests.**

## What Tabwise processes

| Data | Use | Where it's kept |
|---|---|---|
| Titles and addresses of your open tabs | Search, finding duplicates, grouping, deciding which tabs to suspend | Read when needed, held in memory only |
| Saved sessions (addresses, titles, pin and group information) | Reopening tabs later | Your browser's local extension storage |
| Settings and rules | Your preferences | Your browser's local extension storage |

Tabwise doesn't read the content of web pages, and it doesn't read your browsing history beyond the tabs currently open. It doesn't use analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, cookies or accounts. Site icons in the popup come from the browser's local favicon cache.

## Your control

Delete any saved session from the settings page, or turn autosave off. Exported session files are created only when you click *Export*, and saved where you choose. Uninstalling Tabwise deletes all of its stored data.
