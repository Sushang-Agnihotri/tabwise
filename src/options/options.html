        About & privacy

        • Nothing leaves your device. Tabwise has no servers, no analytics and no network requests. Saved sessions and settings stay in your browser's local storage.
        • Why it can see your tabs: searching, finding duplicates and saving sessions need tab titles and addresses. The browser describes this permission as "read your browsing history". Tabwise only reads the tabs you have open.
        • Suspending uses the browser's own tab discarding, so tabs keep their real address and can't be lost if Tabwise is removed.
        • Shortcut: press Alt+Shift+T to open tab search. Change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
